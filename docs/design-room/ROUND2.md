# Round 2 — cross-critique

DESIGN ONLY. Edit nothing outside `docs/design-room/`. No npm, servers or builds.

Read `BRIEF.md`, then all three round-1 proposals: `round1-claude.md`, `round1-deepseek.md`, `round1-mimo.md`.

Write your round-2 file (path given in your instruction). Max ~1,200 words. Required sections:

1. **Steal list**: the 3-5 best ideas from the OTHER two proposals that you would adopt, naming whose idea it is and why it beats yours.
2. **Attack list**: the 3-5 weakest or riskiest ideas across ALL proposals (including your own). For each: what breaks (perf, mobile, clarity, contract, cost) and a concrete fix or cut.
3. **Disagreements**: where proposals contradict each other (hero technique, section order, type, motion rules). Pick a side and give the deciding reason.
4. **Merged hero**: your final hero storyboard, 0-10 s, plus the static first paint and the 375px version, in at most 12 lines.
5. **Merged motion language and type scale**: final numbers only.

Be blunt. Do not flatter. Do not restate your round-1 proposal.
