## 2. Story engine (new)
`src/lib/stories.ts` holds the data below verbatim. `src/components/story/StoryScene.tsx` renders it. The wide layout uses `hidden md:block` and the tall layout `md:hidden`, the same CSS-only switch as the hero.
- **Server render = final frame.** Everything is visible, and elements with `final: false` are omitted. Without JS or with reduced motion, the reader sees the finished drawing.
- **Client.** When the visible SVG is 35% or more in view and motion is allowed, `gsap` is imported dynamically. The engine builds one timeline: `repeat: -1`, `repeatDelay: 1.6`, every tween via `fromTo` so each loop resets. It pauses when out of view or when `document.hidden` is true. It sets `data-playing="true|false"` on the `<svg>`.
- **Step verbs:**
  - `in`: opacity 0→1 and y 6→0, 0.4 s. On `rows`, the children stagger by `stagger` (default 0.1).
  - `draw`: stroke-dashoffset 1→0 with `pathLength=1`, 0.5 s. On `region`, the rect is drawn.
  - `move`: a token travels along `along` from `getPointAtLength(0)` to the end (proxy t 0→1 setting cx and cy), 0.6 s. Opacity is 1 while moving and 0 at the end.
  - `flash`: the element's fill goes to `#FFE1D2` and back, 0.5 s.
  - `pop`: scale 0.6→1 and opacity 0→1 around its centre, 0.3 s.
  - `out`: opacity →0, 0.2 s.
  - `grow`: bars scaleY 0→1 from the bottom with a 0.08 s stagger. A `progress` fill scales X 0→1 from the left, with duration `dur`.
  - `type`: clip-path `inset(0 100% 0 0)` → `inset(0 0 0 0)`, 0.6 s.
  - `spin`: the clock hand rotates 0→360° around the centre, duration `dur`.
  - `sweep`: the scan line moves y from top to bottom of its rect, duration `dur`.
- **Visual style** (CSS in index.css under `.story`). Font: mono.
  - `box`: rect fill #FFFFFF, stroke ink 1.25, rx 3. Label 11px ink at (x+10, y+18). Sub 9px faint at (x+10, y+32).
  - `path`: stroke ink 1.25, fill none. `dashed` uses 4 4.
  - `doc`: white rect with a folded corner. `lines` count of 1px `line`-coloured rules.
  - `region`: dashed 3 3 rect in flame, with a label 9px flame above its top-left.
  - `token`: circle r 4.5, fill flame.
  - `stamp`: r 10 circle. `check` is an ink fill with a white tick. `cross` is a flame fill with a white x.
  - `bars`: n equal bars with a 6px gap, fill ink, heights = value×h.
  - `rows`: n rects of height rowH−4, fill panel.
  - `progress`: track rect stroke line, plus a fill rect of flame.
  - `text`: size default 10, tone ink, muted or accent (flame).
  - `clock`: circle stroke ink, 12 o'clock tick, hand line.
  - `bubble`: rect rx 10. `right` is an ink fill with white text. `left` is a white fill with ink text. Text is 10px at x+12, vertically centred.
  - `scan`: a 2px flame rect across the width at the top of its rect.
- **Captions.** Under each story, a one-line caption is shown in the card, in mono 11px muted.

## Review amendments (Sonnet design + eng review, 2026-10-19). These override the sections above.

**Coordinates**: all collision fixes are already applied to the data above, and `scripts/stories.test.mts` enforces them (8 checks: viewBox bounds, label fit, stamp and box overlap, unique ids, verb variety).

**Engine**
1. **No blank-flash.** SSR renders the final frame. When a scene first reaches 35% in view, the engine fades every animated target to its start state over 0.25 s, then plays. Wrap the build in a try/catch; any error leaves the final frame in place.
2. **No duplicate DOM ids.** Elements carry `data-sid="<id>"`, never `id`. Queries are scoped to the scene's own `<svg>`.
3. **Animate only the visible layout.** `matchMedia('(min-width: 768px)')` picks wide or tall. Observe and animate only that `<svg>`. On a breakpoint change, kill the timeline and rebuild.
4. **One gsap import.** `src/motion/load-gsap.ts` exports `loadGsap = () => import('gsap').then((m) => m.gsap ?? m.default)`, memoised in a module variable. Use `gsap.context(() => ..., svg)` and `ctx.revert()` on cleanup.
5. **Dashed paths** (`dashed: true`) do not dash-draw. Their `draw` verb is an opacity fade from 0 to 1. Solid paths use `pathLength="1"` with stroke-dasharray 1 and dashoffset. Token motion uses `path.getTotalLength() * t` with `getPointAtLength`.
6. **Covered cards pause.** While a scene is intersecting, check every 400 ms whether `document.elementFromPoint(centre of svg)` is inside that svg. If it is not, because a stacked card covers it, pause. Resume when it is uncovered again. Also pause on `document.hidden`, and kill the timeline on unmount.
7. **Tokens** render at cx=0, cy=0 and move with gsap `x`/`y`.
   - `pop` uses `transformOrigin: 'center center'` with `svgOrigin`-free `transform-box: fill-box` (CSS).
   - `flash` animates the opacity of a per-element overlay rect (`.st-flash`, fill #FFE1D2), not the fill itself.
8. **Accessibility.** Each `<svg>` has `role="img"` and `aria-label` set to the story caption. Watch reduced-motion changes: if reduce turns on, kill the timeline and show the final frame.
